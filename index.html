1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to LoopCount. This Privacy Policy describes how LoopCount ("we", "us", or "our") protects your privacy when using our Android application.

Core Privacy Guarantee: LoopCount is an offline-first utility. We do NOT collect, transmit, share, or sell any personal data, audio files, or usage telemetry to any external servers.

2. Information We Handle

  • Local Audio Files: The app accesses audio files stored locally on your device (MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, AAC, OGG) solely to play and loop them. Files are never uploaded to any cloud server.
  • Loop Presets & History: Loop markers (A-B points), target count numbers, and playlist configurations are stored locally on your device via an encrypted SQLite database.
  • No User Accounts: The app requires no login, email, phone number, or credentials.

3. Android System Permissions

LoopCount requests only the strictly required Android permissions:

READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Allows you to select and play your locally stored music tracks.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE & MEDIA_PLAYBACK Keeps audio looping active when the screen is locked or while using other apps.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Displays the lock-screen player and notification controls on Android 13+.
WAKE_LOCK Prevents system sleep during active loop rehearsal sessions.

4. Third-Party Services

LoopCount does not contain any third-party ad networks, advertising identifiers, or tracking SDKs. It uses standard AndroidX libraries created by Google for media playback and UI layout.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

LoopCount does not collect personal data from anyone, making it safe and compliant for all ages, including students and children.
